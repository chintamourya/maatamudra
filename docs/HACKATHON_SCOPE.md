# MAATAMUDRA hackathon scope and judging alignment

## Product statement

MAATAMUDRA is an open-source Indian contract intelligence prototype that helps citizens, advocates and businesses create, understand, review and negotiate common agreements using conversational AI and explainable legal issue spotting.

## Judging alignment

The submission pack currently records the event rubric as AI Utilization Depth (25%), Product Innovation (20%), Execution Speed (15%), Startup Viability (20%), UI/UX (10%) and Pitch & Storytelling (10%). Re-check the live official event page before submission; do not substitute a different rubric in the pitch.

| Rubric dimension | Demonstrable capability |
|---|---|
| AI utilization | Structured multilingual intake, upload analysis, grounded Q&A, two-sided review and explainability |
| Innovation | Balanced redlines, deterministic checks, consequence simulation and India-aware legal context |
| Execution speed | Fast template compilation, bounded retrieval and immediate deterministic checks |
| Viability | Citizen, advocate and MSME workflows with a lawyer-in-the-loop model |
| UX | Conversational creation, accessible explanations and visible verification status |
| Pitch | One freelancer/vendor agreement story from intake through review and export |

## Explicit hackathon boundaries

- No government certification or licence is claimed or required merely to publish this open-source drafting prototype.
- No automated government registration is implemented.
- No CCA eSign service is claimed. Any signature-provider flow shown in a demo must be labelled simulated unless connected to an actual provider under its terms.
- No universal stamp-duty calculator is claimed.
- All 36 Indian States/UTs are selectable, but state-specific rules are not all curated or verified.
- Legal findings are issue-spotting prompts, not legal advice or enforceability determinations.
- The existing nine contract starters are retained; the hackathon build focuses on improving quality rather than adding many weak templates.

## Demo path

1. Choose Citizen, Advocate or Business workspace mode.
2. Describe a freelance/service or rental arrangement in English, Hindi or Telugu.
3. Complete the missing structured fields and generate the agreement.
4. Open India law and show central-law prompts plus transparent state coverage.
5. Run two-sided negotiation and compare the balanced redline.
6. Open the time machine or ask a question about the contract.
7. Explain a clause in the user's language and export the DOCX.

## Benchmark protocol

Prepare 20–30 synthetic or redacted sample clauses. Have a qualified reviewer label expected missing clauses, contradictions, statutory issue prompts and extraction fields. Report precision/recall and citation accuracy only after running the benchmark. Record false positives and missed issues as well as successful detections.
